# Device matrix

Accuracy status below is what has actually been **measured**. No tier has been validated against tape/laser ground truth yet, so no assignment gate is claimed for any of them (see [submission_status.md](submission_status.md)).

| Tier | Minimum hardware | Required sensor / data | Expected accuracy status | Known limitations |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Photos** | iPhone 15 or newer (any phone camera that gives sharp JPEGs works in principle) | RGB stills only: 2-8 per room, folder per room. No poses, no depth. | **Widest uncertainty.** Metric scale comes from a monocular depth model (typically 10-15% off in earlier checks against ARKit poses on video footage). Real benchmark **NOT VALIDATED**: only synthetic and mocked tests exist. | Weak SfM on textureless or low-overlap rooms; rooms with no shared view or doorway photographed from both sides stay unplaced; few photos mean open wall loops; scale is per room and not forced to agree. |
| **Video** | iPhone 15 or newer | One continuous RGB walkthrough video (1x rear camera). | **Wider than LiDAR.** SfM trajectory (PyCOLMAP) plus monocular metric depth for scale; every length carries a propagated relative sigma (about 10% floor on scale). Real walkthrough **NOT VALIDATED**: one real run on a LiDAR scanning-pass video gave PARTIAL with weak tracking (24 of 145 keyframes registered). | COLMAP is not bit-reproducible; fast sweeps, white walls and blur fragment SfM; only the largest connected model is used; fused monocular clouds are noisier than LiDAR so fewer walls and rooms close. |
| **LiDAR** | LiDAR-equipped iPhone Pro or iPad Pro | Depth maps (16-bit), poses, intrinsics; RGB video recommended. | **Strongest tier.** Measured depth and poses; intervals from the stages' own uncertainty. Validated for execution and determinism on 3 sample captures; **dimensional accuracy NOT VALIDATED** (no tape/laser truth). | Odometry drift (pose correction is accepted only when metrics justify it); walls blurred to a few centimetres; most opening heights unobserved; glass and mirrors; furniture can pass as walls. |
| **Damage layer** (all tiers) | none beyond the tier's RGB | RGB evidence with poses | Deterministic OpenCV crack/stain **candidates**; no learned model; precision and recall **NOT MEASURED**. | Furniture, shadows and cables look like damage; metric extents inherit the surface's uncertainty; concealed-damage flags are rule-based risk indicators, never assertions. |

Software requirements (all tiers): Windows or other desktop OS, **Python 3.11**, **CPU only** (no GPU). `python -m spatialforge doctor` reports which tiers are ready on a machine.
